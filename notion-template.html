With a live word of the day built in

Each template has an advanced word of the day card for its exam: medical words for the OET, legal words for TOLES, academic words for IELTS. It changes every few hours, so your dashboard teaches you a word every time you open it. Want it on another page? Build your own widget.

Questions

Are the templates free?
Yes, completely. Open one, press Duplicate in the top right corner of Notion, and it is yours. A free Notion account is enough.
Which template should a doctor or nurse use?
The OET Exam Prep System. It is built around the four OET sub-tests and the referral letter, and the case log is organised by specialty.
Does the vocabulary log work offline on my phone?
It works in the Notion app like any other page. For vocabulary you see without opening anything, Lexfall puts the words on your iPhone Lock Screen.
