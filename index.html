How it works

Words that fall into place

For people whose English is a gate to something: a licence, a job, a client, an exam. If beginner apps feel too easy, this is the next step. And if English is already your first language, it's 20,000 words worth knowing to sharpen how you think and write.

01 · Day one

Pick your level. Take a guided first lesson.

One tap: Intermediate, Advanced or Expert. Not sure? Check with a few words instead. Then a guided first lesson with two words and gentle checks, a finger showing you how, and Day 1 is done.

  • No test to sit before you start
  • Sound and hints in every lesson
  • Change your level any time
02 · Your daily lesson

A few minutes. Words that stay.

A short lesson every day: a few new words, each with its meaning, a real example and how it sounds, then a quick check. Spaced review brings every word back just before you would forget it.

  • Pronunciation in British, American and other accents
  • Save a word to your notebook in one tap
  • Choose Practical, Balanced or Rare words
03 · Journey

A path that climbs with you.

Your Journey is a path of lessons. Each one you finish moves you further up, from your level towards Expert. Add a path for a field or an exam and they climb side by side.

Questions

Before you download.

Is Lexfall free?

Lexfall is free to download and starts with a free trial. After the trial, a subscription (monthly or annual) unlocks every professional field, academic track and premium feature. It renews automatically, and you can cancel any time in your App Store account settings.

Which devices does it run on?

Lexfall is built for iPhone and needs iOS 15.1 or later. Android is coming to Google Play soon: join the waitlist and we will email you when it is ready.

What level do I need?

Lexfall is for intermediate to expert learners (B2 to C2) and for native speakers who want more range. You pick your level in one tap: Intermediate, Advanced or Expert. Not sure? Tap the words you know and Lexfall places you, so you start on words you don't already know.

Which fields does it cover?

General, Healthcare, Law, Business and New Words (the language still being coined), plus word lists for 15 exams: 20,000+ words in total, organized by field rather than by a vague level.

How does the widget work?

A Home Screen and Lock Screen widget shows a fresh word every few hours, so you meet new vocabulary without opening the app. Spaced review brings each word back before you forget it.

Is it a full exam course?

No, it is a vocabulary tool, not an exam course. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the organisations behind OET, IELTS, Cambridge English, TOLES or GRE.

How does a lesson work?

One short lesson a day. Each word comes with its meaning, a real example and its sound, then gentle checks with hints. Spaced review brings every word back just before you would forget it. Your first lesson is guided, two words and a few checks, so you see how it works.

Something is not working. Who do I contact?

Write to us from the Support page and we will reply by email.
