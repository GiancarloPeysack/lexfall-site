Questions

Before you download.

What is Lexfall?

Lexfall is a daily lesson path for advanced English (C1-C2), on iPhone and Android. Short lessons teach the words that matter for your work, your exam or everyday life, then smart reviews make them stick. Pick your level and your field (healthcare, law, business or general English) or one of 15 exams, and you climb lesson by lesson from your level up to Expert. Between lessons, a swipe feed and Home and Lock Screen widgets keep new words in front of you. It is free to download on the App Store and Google Play.

How does a lesson work?

Each short lesson on your Journey gives you a few new words, each with its meaning, a real example and its sound, then a quick check with hints. Smart reviews bring every word back later, just before you would forget it. Each lesson you finish moves you further up the path, from your level towards Expert. Your first lesson is guided: two words and a few checks, so you see how it works.

What level do I need?

Lexfall is for upper-intermediate to expert learners who want C1 and C2 English, and for native speakers who want more range. You pick your level in one tap: Intermediate, Advanced or Expert. Not sure? Check with a few words instead, so you start on words you don't already know. Your path starts at your level, your first lesson is guided, and you can change your level any time.

Is Lexfall free?

Lexfall is free to download and starts with a free trial; the App Store or Google Play shows its length before you subscribe. After the trial, a subscription (monthly or annual) unlocks every field, exam list and premium feature. It renews automatically, and you can cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Which fields and exams does it cover?

Five fields: General, Healthcare, Law, Business and New Words (the language still being coined). Fifteen exams: IELTS, TOEFL, TOEIC, OET, PLAB, TOLES, PTE Academic, Duolingo English Test, Cambridge C1 Advanced, Cambridge C2 Proficiency, GRE Verbal, Linguaskill Business, Michigan ECPE, LanguageCert C1/C2 and Trinity ISE. 20,000+ words in total.

Is it a full exam course?

No, it is a vocabulary tool, not an exam course: it does not coach the test or mark your work. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the organisations behind any of the exams it lists, such as IELTS, TOEFL, OET, Cambridge English, TOLES or GRE.

More questions (7)
What is a good app for advanced English vocabulary?

If beginner apps feel too easy, you need one that starts where they stop. Lexfall is a daily lesson path for advanced English (C1-C2): each short lesson teaches a few words with their meaning, a real example sentence and their pronunciation, checks them, and brings them back with smart reviews so they stay. The path is built for your level and your field (general English, healthcare, law, business or new words) or your exam. Whatever app you use, reading widely in English and noting the words you meet is still the best partner to it.

Is there an app for IELTS vocabulary?

Yes. Lexfall has word lists for 15 exams, including IELTS, TOEFL, OET, PTE Academic, TOEIC, Cambridge C1 Advanced and C2 Proficiency, TOLES and PLAB. Add an exam and your daily lessons and checks pull words from its list. You can browse free sample lists on our word lists page, starting with IELTS Academic vocabulary. Lexfall teaches vocabulary only; it is not a full IELTS course.

How do I learn C1 and C2 words?

A few at a time, in context, and reviewed over weeks. Learn each word with its meaning, a real example sentence and its sound. Check yourself on it a little later, then review it again just before you would forget it (spaced review). Use it in your own writing or speech within a day or two. Lexfall builds these steps into short daily lessons on a path, so you do not have to plan them.

Which devices does it run on?

Lexfall runs on iPhone (iOS 15.1 or later) and on Android phones through Google Play.

Can I add words I find elsewhere?

Yes. Select a word in Safari or another app, tap Share, then Add to Lexfall, and it goes straight into your notebook. If Lexfall does not have it yet, it is saved as your own word with your meaning. You can export your notebook as text or CSV.

How does the widget work?

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show a fresh word every few hours, so new words stay in front of you between lessons. On iOS 17 and later you can save the word or skip to the next one right from the widget.

Something is not working. Who do I contact?

Write to us from the Support page and we will reply by email.
