An advanced word of the day for your Notion dashboard

A quiet, beautiful card that shows one advanced English word, its meaning and a real example. It changes every few hours. Pick medical, legal, business or exam vocabulary, choose a style, copy the link, paste it into Notion. Free, no sign-up, no tracking.

Live preview. This is exactly what appears in Notion.

Add it to Notion in 20 seconds

1. Copy

Build your widget above and press Copy.

2. Embed

In any Notion page type /embed, press Enter, paste the link and choose Embed link.

3. Resize

Drag the corner to about 300 px tall. It looks great in a 2 or 3 column dashboard row. Pick Clear to make it blend into your page.

Use it anywhere

It is a plain web page, so it works in Figma (with any iframe or embed plugin), Obsidian, Coda, Craft, a website or a study blog. Paste this snippet:

<iframe src="https://lexfall.app/widget?cat=exam" width="100%" height="300" style="border:0;border-radius:16px" title="Advanced word of the day"></iframe>

Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. No account, no ads, no cookies, no limits. The word list is a static file, so the widget loads instantly and keeps working.
Where do the words come from?
From Lexfall's corpus of 20,000+ advanced words, written and checked by hand, graded B2 to C2. Medical words lean on the OET, legal words on TOLES, exam words on IELTS, TOEFL, GRE and Cambridge C2.
Does it follow Notion dark mode?
Choose Auto and it follows your device theme, which is what Notion follows by default.
Can I build a whole study system around it?
Yes. Our free OET, TOLES and IELTS Notion templates already have it built in.
