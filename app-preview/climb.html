Journey as a climb

A browser sketch of the Journey tab as a 2.5D path. Scroll inside the phone: up climbs, down goes back toward where you started.

Theme
Time of day
  • No visible end. The path always runs over the next rise and fades into haze, so it reads as a path, not a map.
  • Places along the way. Each unit is a place the path walks through: the village, the market square, the station, the library, a mountain hut, the observatory. A signpost with the unit name stands at each entrance.
  • Through the mountains. A stream with a plank bridge, switchbacks up a slope, a pass between rock walls and a tunnel into the alpine valley.
  • The view changes as you climb. The camera rises and tilts from the ground toward the sky.
  • Its own time of day. The scenery follows the phone's clock (day, golden hour, moonlit night with lit windows), not the app theme. Theme only changes the glass, tabs and stops. Use the preview control to compare.
  • Scroll cue. The view nudges up once and "Scroll to climb" shows until the first scroll. "Back to today" appears when your lesson is off screen.
  • Pro track. A fainter side path branches off to the clinic (Medical track). In the app, tapping it opens the paywall.
  • Tap targets. Nearby stops and the Start bubble never take less than 44 pt to tap.
  • Reduce Motion: no tilt change, no glide, no pulse, no bob. Preview it with ?reduce=1.
