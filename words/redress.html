redress

/rɪˈdrɛs/

C1CEFR level
60/100Rarity: rare
FormalRegister

General noun

Remedy or compensation for a wrong or grievance.

The families sought redress through the courts after the disaster.

Similar: reparation, remedy, restitution

Origin: From Old French redresser (to set upright again).

Topic: remedy in General English

Law noun

The remedying of a wrong, or the means by which a wrong is set right.

Victims of the abuse were left without any effective redress.

Similar: remedy

Topic: Human rights in Legal English

Business noun

A remedy or compensation for a wrong or grievance suffered at work.

The grievance procedure gives staff a formal route to seek redress.

Similar: remedy

Topic: Human resources in Business English
