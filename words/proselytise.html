proselytise

verb/ˈprɒsɪlɪtaɪz/

C1CEFR level
68/100Rarity: rare
NeutralRegister

To try actively to convert others to one's opinion, faith or cause.

He never dined with us without proselytising for his new diet.

Similar: evangelise, convert
