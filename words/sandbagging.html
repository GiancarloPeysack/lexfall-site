sandbagging

/ˈsændˌbæɡɪŋ/

C1CEFR level
80/100Rarity: very rare
TechnicalRegister

Law noun

Closing a deal despite already knowing a representation is false, then suing on the breach afterwards.

The buyer's counsel demanded a pro-sandbagging clause so a defect they already knew about would still support an indemnity claim after completion.

Topic: Corporate law in Legal English

Business noun

A buyer's right to close a deal and then claim indemnity for warranty breaches it already knew about at signing.

Their counsel slipped in an anti-sandbagging line, which guts our indemnity for anything we spotted in diligence.

Similar: pro-sandbagging

Topic: Deals in Business English
