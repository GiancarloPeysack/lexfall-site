legal advice privilege

noun/ˈliːɡəl ədˈvaɪs ˈprɪvəlɪdʒ/

C2CEFR level
85/100Rarity: very rare
TechnicalRegister

The limb of legal professional privilege protecting confidential lawyer-client communications made for the purpose of giving or obtaining legal advice, whether or not litigation is contemplated.

Because no litigation was in prospect, only legal advice privilege could be invoked over the compliance file.
