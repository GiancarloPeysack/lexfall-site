jettison

/ˈdʒetɪsən/

C1CEFR level
70/100Rarity: rare
FormalRegister

General verb

To discard or abandon something as no longer wanted or useful.

The party quietly jettisoned the pledge once the election was won.

Similar: discard, ditch, abandon

Topic: Cambridge C1 Advanced in General English

Law noun

The deliberate throwing overboard of cargo or ship's equipment to lighten or save the vessel in time of peril.

The jettison of the heaviest containers was a classic general average sacrifice for the common safety.

Topic: Shipping & maritime in Legal English

Business verb

To throw cargo overboard, or more broadly to discard something as an encumbrance.

The crew jettisoned part of the deck cargo to keep the listing vessel afloat.

Similar: discard, ditch

Topic: Operations & supply chain in Business English
