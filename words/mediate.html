mediate

/ˈmiːdieɪt/

B1CEFR level
22/100Rarity: fairly common
TechnicalRegister

Law verb

To help opposing parties reach their own agreement without imposing a decision.

She was engaged to mediate the licensing row, steering both sides toward a workable compromise.

Similar: conciliate, facilitate, intercede

Topic: Arbitration in Legal English

Business verb

To act as a neutral party helping two sides reach agreement.

A retired judge was brought in to mediate once the parties reached deadlock on damages.

Similar: arbitrate, intercede, broker

Topic: Negotiation in Business English
