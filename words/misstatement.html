misstatement

/ˌmɪsˈsteɪtmənt/

B1CEFR level
55/100Rarity: uncommon
TechnicalRegister

Law noun

An untrue assertion of fact made in the course of dealing.

A negligent misstatement in the prospectus exposed the issuer to liability.

Similar: misrepresentation, misdescription

Topic: Contracts in Legal English

Business noun

A difference between a reported figure and the amount required by the applicable reporting framework.

The aggregated misstatement remained below materiality, so no adjustment to the accounts was demanded.

Similar: error, inaccuracy

Topic: Accounting in Business English
