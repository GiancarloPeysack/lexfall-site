annulment

noun/əˈnʌlmənt/

C1CEFR level
58/100Rarity: uncommon
TechnicalRegister

The judicial vacating of an arbitral award, rendering it of no legal effect.

Annulment is available only on narrow grounds such as a denial of due process.

Similar: vacatur
