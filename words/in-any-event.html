in any event

phrase

B1CEFR level
18/100Rarity: common
TechnicalRegister

A connector introducing an argument that holds regardless of whether the preceding point succeeds.

The notice was valid; in any event, any defect was waived by the Defendant's subsequent conduct.

Similar: in any case
