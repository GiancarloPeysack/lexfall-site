damages in lieu of an injunction

noun/ˈdamɪdʒɪz ɪn ˈljuː əv ən ɪnˈdʒʌŋkʃən/

C2CEFR level
82/100Rarity: very rare
TechnicalRegister

A monetary award granted in equity as a substitute for restraining or compelling conduct.

Because an injunction would have been oppressive, the court awarded damages in lieu of an injunction for the trifling encroachment.

Similar: equitable damages

Topic: Litigation in Legal English
