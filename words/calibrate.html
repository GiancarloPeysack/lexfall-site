calibrate

/ˈkælɪbreɪt/

B2CEFR level
45/100Rarity: uncommon
NeutralRegister

General verb

To adjust an instrument or model against a known standard so that its readings are accurate.

Technicians calibrate the balance each morning against a certified reference mass.

Similar: adjust

Topic: Science & reasoning in General English

Business verb

To adjust or fine-tune something so that it is accurate or appropriate.

The team calibrated its cost-saving projections against three independent industry benchmarks before presenting them.

Similar: fine-tune, adjust

Topic: Consulting in Business English
