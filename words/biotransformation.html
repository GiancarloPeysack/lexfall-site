biotransformation

noun/ˌbaɪəʊˌtrænsfəˈmeɪʃən/

C2CEFR level
92/100Rarity: very rare
TechnicalRegister

The chemical alteration of a drug by the body's enzymes into metabolites.

Hepatic biotransformation was impaired in the patient with advanced cirrhosis, prolonging the drug's effect.

Similar: metabolism

Topic: Pharmacology in Medical English
