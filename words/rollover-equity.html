rollover equity

C2CEFR level
90/100Rarity: very rare
TechnicalRegister

Law noun

The portion of sale proceeds that selling owners reinvest as a stake in the buyer's post-deal entity.

The sponsor required thirty percent rollover equity so management kept real skin in the game after the buyout.

Topic: Corporate law in Legal English

Business noun

The stake selling owners reinvest into the buyer's structure rather than cashing out in full.

We required 20% rollover equity so management still has real skin in the game after close.

Similar: equity rollover

Topic: Corporate finance in Business English
