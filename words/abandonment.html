abandonment

/əˈband(ə)nmənt/

B2CEFR level
30/100Rarity: fairly common
TechnicalRegister

Law noun

The relinquishment of a contract or right, often inferred from a party's conduct.

Prolonged mutual inactivity led the tribunal to find an abandonment of the arbitration agreement.

Similar: relinquishment, renunciation

Topic: Contracts in Legal English

Business noun

The surrender by the insured of the remains of insured property to the insurer in order to claim for a total loss.

Notice of abandonment was given so the owner could recover as for a constructive total loss of the vessel.

Topic: Insurance & risk in Business English
