manual muscle testing

noun

C2CEFR level
85/100Rarity: very rare
TechnicalRegister

A clinical assessment in which the strength of a muscle is graded by the resistance it can hold against the examiner's hand.

Manual muscle testing graded his ankle dorsiflexors at three out of five, meaning movement against gravity but not resistance.

Similar: MMT

Topic: Physiotherapy in Medical English
