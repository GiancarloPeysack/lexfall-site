escalation

/ˌɛskəˈleɪʃn/

B1CEFR level
45/100Rarity: uncommon
TechnicalRegister

Medicine noun

The process of raising a deteriorating or high-risk patient to more senior staff according to an agreed protocol.

The escalation policy says a NEWS of five triggers an urgent doctor review.

Topic: Handover & SBAR in Medical English

Law noun

A contractual mechanism referring an unresolved dispute up to a higher tier of resolution.

The escalation clause required senior executives to meet before either side could arbitrate.

Similar: referral, intensification

Topic: Arbitration in Legal English

Business noun

The formal referral of an issue, decision or risk to a higher level of authority when it cannot be resolved locally.

If the two leads still can't agree by Friday, the governance process triggers an escalation to the steering committee.

Similar: referral

Topic: Project management in Business English
