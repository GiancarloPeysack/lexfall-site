play devil's advocate

phrase/pleɪ ˈdevlz ˈædvəkət/

A2CEFR level
8/100Rarity: common
TechnicalRegister

To argue an opposing position for the sake of testing an idea rather than out of genuine disagreement.

Just to play devil's advocate, what happens to this plan if the supplier misses the deadline?

Similar: push back, test the argument

Topic: Business email & meetings in Business English
