manageable

adjective/ˈmænɪdʒəbəl/

A2CEFR level
15/100Rarity: common
TechnicalRegister

Able to be dealt with or kept under control without too much difficulty.

Our aim isn't to remove every ache, but to keep the pain at a level that feels manageable for you.

Similar: controllable
