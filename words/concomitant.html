concomitant

/kənˈkɒmɪtənt/

C2CEFR level
70/100Rarity: rare
FormalRegister

General adjective

Naturally accompanying or occurring together with something else.

Rapid growth brought a concomitant rise in inequality.

Similar: attendant, accompanying, associated

Topic: Advanced English in General English

Medicine adjective

Occurring or existing alongside something else, especially as a related effect.

The referral letter noted the concomitant weight loss reported during the same period.

Similar: accompanying, coexisting

Topic: Clinical consultation in Medical English
