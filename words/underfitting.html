underfitting

noun/ˌʌndəˈfɪtɪŋ/

C1CEFR level
75/100Rarity: rare
TechnicalRegister

The modelling error of being too simple to capture the underlying pattern in the data.

A single linear trend was underfitting the seasonal demand, so the forecast missed every holiday spike.

Topic: Data & analytics in Business English
