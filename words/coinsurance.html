coinsurance

noun/ˌkəʊɪnˈʃʊərəns/

C2CEFR level
80/100Rarity: very rare
TechnicalRegister

An arrangement under which the insured or several insurers share a proportion of each loss.

A ninety per cent coinsurance clause left the firm bearing a tenth of every claim itself.
